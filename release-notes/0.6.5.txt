Vayrone PostMaster 0.6.5

New: "Download mail received from" (moving from another mail server, e.g. QLC PostMaster)
- Each external mailbox can download only mail that reached the provider on or after a date.
  Older mail stays at the provider untouched: never downloaded, never deleted.
- Admin > External mailboxes > "Start date for all..." sets the switch-over day for every mailbox
  at once, so Outlook does not get the old mail a second time.
- Moving the date earlier later downloads the mail now in range, once, without duplicates.
