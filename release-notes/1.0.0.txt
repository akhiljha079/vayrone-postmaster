Vayrone PostMaster 1.0.0

The office mail server for companies whose mailboxes are at an external provider
(Gmail, Zoho, GoDaddy, Hostinger, Microsoft 365, cPanel).

- Fetches every employee's provider mail into an office mailbox within seconds (IMAP push or POP3),
  and sends through one provider account with each person's own address.
- Works with Outlook, Thunderbird, phones and webmail, on a LAN password; folders are the same everywhere.
- PostMaster start date: only mail received from the moment PostMaster takes over is downloaded,
  with its original date, so moving from another mail server (e.g. QLC PostMaster) causes no duplicates.
- Spam, virus and dangerous-attachment filtering with quarantine.
- Easy rules: copy all mail of a person, mail from a sender, or mail by words to someone; forwarding.
- Compliance archive by mailbox (Received / Sent), kept even when users delete mail.
- Automatic backups (disk, NAS, cloud), alerts, signed automatic updates.
- Starts with Windows; desktop shortcut; "Vayrone PostMaster status" health check.
